Part-time work for Japanese companies, from wherever you live.

Japanese teams hire remote part-timers for the languages, markets and night hours they can't cover at home. Hanare makes those jobs readable and applyable without fluent Japanese.

  • 訳Posted in Japanese, readable by you. Every listing is translated, and the original is one tap away.
  • 時Hours in your time zone. See whether a shift lands in your afternoon or at 3 a.m.
  • 円Pay in yen and in your currency. Paid monthly through Wise or PayPal under a contractor agreement.
